HR Department - Employee Handbook
==================================

Welcome to our company! This handbook contains important information about employment policies, benefits, and procedures.

Employee Benefits
-----------------

Our comprehensive benefits package includes:
- Health insurance coverage for employees and dependents
- Retirement plans with company matching
- Professional development opportunities
- Flexible work arrangements

Compensation Structure
---------------------

Base salaries are determined by:
- Job role and responsibilities
- Years of experience
- Market rates and industry standards
- Performance evaluations

Example salary ranges (confidential):
- Junior Developer: $60,000 - $80,000
- Senior Developer: $100,000 - $130,000
- Manager: $120,000 - $150,000
- Director: $150,000 - $200,000
